Barbell Bench Press

Primary Target: Chest & Triceps

Instructions

Lie on the flat bench with your eyes under the bar. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width. Unrack the bar, lower it slowly to your mid-chest, and press it back up to full extension.
